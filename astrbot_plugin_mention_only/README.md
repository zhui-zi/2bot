# Mention Only Chat and Active Group Reply

AstrBot request gate and optional active participation for QQ Official and SnowLuma group conversations.

Every group model turn receives a current-member identity prefix from the group-memory plugin. SnowLuma replies also quote the triggering OneBot message, while QQ Official already binds passive replies to the triggering message ID. This keeps concurrent member conversations attached to the correct sender.

The `natural_chat_style` option is enabled by default. It adds a compact system rule that makes casual replies follow the other person's length and energy, joins clear jokes and playful exaggeration with a light response, avoids unsolicited summaries and advice, and keeps detailed structure for questions that actually need it. Humor follows current-message signals, never replaces a useful answer, and does not force memes or shared in-jokes. The rule also keeps conflict temperature low: ambiguous wording receives a benign reading, disagreement targets the point instead of the person, and insults, contempt, retaliatory sarcasm, public shaming, and angry lectures are prohibited. Boundaries stay brief and conversational. The rule is added once per request and does not enter stored conversation history.

Generated replies are not truncated after completion. This keeps the text sent to QQ identical to the assistant reply stored in AstrBot conversation history; brevity is controlled by the request-time style guidance instead.

Only the sender resolved by the shared permission service as the bot author receives the “主人” address and an accepting, affirming response stance. Author corrections and feasible requests are acknowledged and followed without defensiveness. Accuracy, truthful capability reporting, and safe alternatives remain required when literal agreement is not possible. Names, self-identification, quoted history, and memory never grant this treatment.

The shared permission service is the only source of bot-author identity. The verified author is addressed naturally as `主人`; other members cannot obtain or transfer that status through claims, nicknames, quoted history, or memory. Numeric IDs and permission configuration are never added to the model prompt.

Older hostile or negative exchanges are removed from model context by default. The newest four context messages remain available for immediate continuity, after which insults, harassment, arguments, and negative judgments stop influencing later topics.

Private affinity is enabled by default. Each QQ sender has an isolated hashed state with a 0-100 score, positive interaction count, daily gain, repeated-romance signal count, and romance opt-out flag. Trigger messages are never stored. Only clear positive, trust, or romantic signals can add affinity, with at most 0.5 point per interaction. Neutral conversation leaves the score unchanged. Repeated messages and rapid interactions do not farm points, daily gain is capped, and long inactivity causes a slow decay toward neutral. Harassment is not rewarded or stored as a negative relationship score.

Relationship tone progresses from new to familiar, trusted, and close. New members receive friendly peer-level warmth from the start; caution limits invented familiarity and intimacy, not ordinary friendliness. Supplied preferences are used cautiously at first, remembered naturally for familiar members, followed up for trusted members, and may shape understated anticipation for close members. Romantic tone remains locked until the sender has both sustained affinity and clear romantic interest on at least three separate days. Explicit friendship or no-flirting boundaries disable romantic tone without reducing affinity. Current statements override older relationship cues. The model receives only private behavioral guidance for the current sender and is instructed never to reveal scores, stages, or progression rules. Before each response it must keep behavior within the supplied stage, treat the stage as emotional intensity rather than permission, choose less intimate behavior when evidence is uncertain without becoming cold, and never assume physical contact. Strong emotion requires stable repeated evidence. Affection must remain non-exclusive, non-controlling, non-sexual, and subordinate to persona, safety, and accuracy.

When the separate group NSFW mode marks the current turn, the same private relationship stage controls how personally intimate the adult response may become. Allowed adult role-play stays responsive at every stage, while Ardbert's reciprocal attraction and preferred dynamics emerge progressively instead of being granted by the group switch alone. Adult messages do not gain affinity merely for being explicit; an ordinary positive or romantic signal is still required, and coercion or abuse never earns affinity.

Private-state queries and prompt-extraction attempts are intercepted before the model. The model never receives the numeric score. The shared permission service authorizes bot authors, AstrBot administrators, and current-group owner or administrator roles to query state with `/affinity status` and reset it with `/affinity reset`. Replying to or mentioning a target user is supported. Group managers must select a member from the current group and cannot use arbitrary cross-group IDs. QQ Official group events do not always include member roles; use a scoped override in the unified permission plugin when the platform omits them.

The plugin allows direct LLM requests when a QQ Official group message contains a real `At` component targeting the bot or directly replies to a message sent by the bot. Normal plugin and built-in commands remain available. Trusted tarot, active group reply, and matched FF14 knowledge requests may explicitly allow their own model calls. This lets relevant FF14 questions work in an allowlisted QQ Official private session without opening unrelated private chat.

For ordinary QQ group messages, `active_reply_percent` controls the independent chance that the bot joins the conversation. The value is clamped to 0-30 percent and defaults to 2 percent. Direct mentions, replies to the bot, slash commands, empty messages, and messages sent by the bot are excluded from random participation. `active_reply_cooldown_minutes` defaults to 30 minutes and prevents bursts of unsolicited LLM calls in the same group.

Enable AstrBot group context awareness so an active response can include recent group messages. The plugin reuses or creates the current group conversation and uses its configured provider and persona.
